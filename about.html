Biography

I'm Abdulalim, a software engineer based in Nigeria. I started with mobile application development in 2020 and moved into full software engineering work in 2022. Over time my interest shifted toward the systems behind the apps: APIs, databases and how the pieces fit together.

I'm now the CTO and a backend developer at Schoola, an education technology company, where I mostly work with FastAPI and contribute to backend systems and DevOps. Outside of work I build side projects, try out ideas, and keep learning.
